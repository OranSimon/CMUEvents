                        <div>                             <h1>Social Cybersecurity Seminars</h1>
<p>Please join us for the first meeting of the Social Cybersecurity Working Group this semester, where we will be hosting <strong><em>Evan Williams,</em></strong> a fourth-year PhD candidate at Carnegie Mellon University. He will be presenting his research on <strong><em>"Connecting Social Media and Search Engines: Dredge Words and Alt-tech Search Engine Audits.</em></strong></p>
<h5>What: <em>Social Cybersecurity Working Group</em><br/> When: <em>First Thursday of the month, 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM EST</em><br/><br/>Remote Details: <br/><br/>Zoom Connectivity: https://renci.zoom.us/j/98870334048<br/>Meeting ID: 988 7033 4048</h5>
<h5 style="font-weight: 400;"><img alt="Evan Williams" height="260" src="https://www.cmu.edu/casos-center/e_williams.jpg" width="238"/></h5>
<p><strong>Evan Williams<br/>PhD student, Carnegie Mellon University<br/>CMU IDeaS Center<br/></strong></p>
<p><strong style="font-size: 1em;">Bio:&#160;</strong><i style="font-size: 1em;">&#160;</i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Evan Williams is a fourth-year PhD Student at Carnegie Mellon University advised by Dr. Kathleen Carley. He formerly worked with the Department of State's Global Engagement Center on efforts to identify international state-backed disinformation campaigns. His research aims to better understand how users access misinformation on search engines. He also works on building models that can help search engines improve the reliability of returned results.</span></p>
