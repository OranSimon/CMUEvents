                        <div>                             <h2 align="center">&#160;</h2>
<p style="text-align: center;"><img alt="2026silogo" class="" height="542" src="2026_si-logo-resized-for-website1.png" width="1540"/></p>
<div style="text-align: center;"></div>
<div style="text-align: center;"></div>
<h2 style="text-align: center;"><strong>August 10-14, 2026</strong></h2>
<h2 style="text-align: center;"><strong>Carnegie Mellon University<br/>Gates/Hillman Center, 4902 Forbes Ave., Pittsburgh, PA 15213<br/>4<sup>th</sup><span>&#160;</span>Floor, Room 4405</strong></h2>
<p>The <strong>CASOS and IDeaS AI-Enabled Network Analysis Training &#8211; 2026 Summer Institute</strong> provides an introduction to network science and the ways in which artificial intelligence (AI) can enhance network analysis. Topics covered include the identification of key actors and groups, stance detection, network comparison, and network dynamics. Participants will also explore how AI can be used to generate synthetic network data, label groups, and identify missing links within networks.</p>
<p>The training highlights the complementary strengths of network science and AI, demonstrating how network science can help overcome limitations in AI systems, and how AI can address gaps in network data and support more robust analysis. Much of the instruction is hands-on. Participants will be provided with data and analytical tools and will work through scenario-based exercises throughout the week, producing insights using AI-enabled network science methods.</p>
<p>The <strong>AI-Enabled Network Analysis Training &#8211; 2026 Summer Institute</strong> is a five-day workshop, held <strong>Monday, August 10th through Friday, August 14th</strong><span>,</span>&#160;with daily instruction from <strong>8:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. (U.S. Eastern Time)</strong>. In-person attendance is strongly preferred; however, a limited access hybrid participation option is available. Trial licenses for all required tools will be provided during the training. In-person participants will work in small groups of two to three to develop a final presentation (e.g., PowerPoint slides). Group coordination will not be provided for remote participants. This workshop is a <strong>certificate program</strong>. Participants who attend at least 80% of the sessions, actively engage in the hands-on activities, and complete a final presentation demonstrating their learning will receive a certificate of completion.</p>
<p><strong>Curriculum: <a href="_2026_summer_institute_agenda3.pdf">Agenda</a>&#160;</strong></p>
<p>The hands-on curriculum builds on both social network and computational analysis techniques, and illustrates how to use these techniques to study social, organizational and policy issues. Final agenda will be distributed to registered participants in advance of instruction on August 10.</p>
<p><strong>Tools:</strong></p>
<p>All participants will be given trial software for use during Summer Institute and a trial period following the event.</p>

<ul>
<li>Kathleen M. Carley, PhD, Carnegie Mellon University, Director of CASOS and IDeaS Centers</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Registration&#160;</strong></p>
<p><strong><em>Registration is now open on Regpack.&#160;</em></strong><em>Registration fees for&#160;<strong>AI-Enabled Network Analysis Training &#8211; Summer Institute</strong>&#160;<strong>2026&#160;</strong>are posted below.&#160;</em>Participation is open to graduate students and post docs, faculty, and personnel from industry, education, and government.&#160;</p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><em><strong>International Access Notice:&#160;&#160;</strong></em><span>Participants registering from outside the United States may experience issues accessing the registration system due to regional network restrictions. If you encounter any difficulties, please switch to a U.S.-based VPN connection and try again. If the issues continues after doing so, please contact us for assistance.</span></p>
<p><span><strong>&#160;To</strong> </span><strong>register go to the 2026 Summer Institute&#160;&#160;</strong><strong><em><a href="http://www.regpack.com/reg/summerinstitute2026">Registration page.</a></em></strong></p>
<p>&#160;<strong>In-person participants</strong>&#160;will receive hands-on support with software installation and enjoy valuable networking opportunities with CASOS and IDeaS Center faculty, staff, students, and fellow attendees.</p>
<p><strong>Virtual participants&#160;</strong>will only have access to live-streamed talks via Zoom.</p>
<p>&#160;<strong>Registration fees:&#160;In-person or virtual attendance</strong></p>
<p>Students/Postdocs: $960.00</p>
<p>Faculty / Government / Military (with ID) / Industry: $1,260.00</p>

<p data-end="1510" data-start="1347">If you have any food allergies or dietary restrictions, we are happy to accommodate them. Please be sure to note any allergies or restrictions during registration.</p>
