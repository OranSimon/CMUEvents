                                                           <h1>Make Research Come Alive in the Humanities, Arts &amp; Social Sciences</h1>
                                            <p><span style="font-weight: 400;"><a href="https://www.cmu.edu/history/cause/" rel="noopener" target="_blank"></a></span><span style="font-weight: 400;">Join Edda Fields-Black and leading scholars at the </span><strong style="font-size: 1em;">3rd annual Spring Humanities, Arts &amp; Social Sciences (HASS) Symposium</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> on </span><strong style="font-size: 1em;">Thursday, April 17, 2025,</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> at the </span><strong style="font-size: 1em;">William Pitt Union</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;">. Learn how HASS researchers have taken their work &#8220;off the shelves,&#8221; reached broader audiences, and gained national recognition. Local funding organizations will also share what makes HASS research stand out.</span></p>
<p><strong>Speakers:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li aria-level="1" style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Edda Fields-Black</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> (CMU) &#8211; History and Humanities Center Director</span></li>
<li aria-level="1" style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Marcus Rediker</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> (Pitt) &#8211; Distinguished Professor of History</span></li>
<li aria-level="1" style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Jeanne-Marie Laskas</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> (Pitt) &#8211; Distinguished Professor of English</span></li>
<li aria-level="1" style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Erin Anderson</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> (Pitt) &#8211; Assistant Professor of English</span></li>
</ul>
