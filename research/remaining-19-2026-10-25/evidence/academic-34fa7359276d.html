Socialist Studies Seminar

The Socialist Studies Seminar is an interdisciplinary seminar with faculty and graduate student participation from the greater Pittsburgh area. It is devoted to the study of socialist states and movements throughout the world. It meets once a month to discuss readings, the scholarly work of its participants, films, and presentations from invited speakers.
