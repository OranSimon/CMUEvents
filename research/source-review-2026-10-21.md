## What limited extraction

- **Missing or conflicting dates:** Chess and Juntos omit an event year; DSSC review pages disagree about the year. A copyright year, publication date, video upload or arbitrary recurrence bound cannot establish an occurrence.
- **Image and layout dependence:** LTI and Socialist Studies publish schedules as poster images. LTI OCR was inspected but confused rotated text and speaker/date pairing. The music PDF is supported only for explicitly dated recital sections after rendering and visual inspection; colored class and cancellation grids are excluded.
- **Authenticated archives:** the four Mailman sources advertise private archives. A public subscription landing page does not make its messages public.
- **Remaining extractor gaps:** some directories, on-demand offerings, undated recording archives and heterogeneous announcement sections do not yield dependable event records. The per-source rows distinguish absent information from an extractor that still needs work.
- **Repaired implementation problems:** relative newsletter issue links, inspected email layouts, scoped semester schedules, public embedded calendars, exact empty-widget notices, old ICS anomalies and non-web event URLs. Unknown timezones or potentially current incomplete records remain visible failures.
- **Upstream TLS configuration:** Eberly omitted an intermediate certificate. Its reviewed public intermediate is now supplied for that exact host, checked against an existing trusted root. Certificate and hostname validation remain enabled.

LTI illustrates the difference between a page limitation and missing events: its full poster schedule remains unsupported, while the official SCS calendar independently supplies the Sewon Min colloquium on October 23, 2026, 3:30–5:00 PM Eastern. That overlapping event does not establish complete coverage of the LTI series.

The scoped SCS Distinguished Lecture JSON endpoint is used because its ICS export ignores the equivalent filter. Matching Trumba event identifiers preserve deduplication against the broader official calendar. A successfully parsed historical schedule outside the rolling window is a valid empty result.
