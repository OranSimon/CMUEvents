Oren Etzioni :
https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~etzioni/site/is a pioneering Artificial Intelligence researcher, entrepreneur, and prominent voice in AI safety and ethics.

Oren was the Founding Chief Executive Officer at the Allen Institute for AI (AI2) from its inception in 2013 until late 2022 - one of the most highly respected AI research institutes in the world, whose mission is AI for the Common Good. Under his leadership, AI2 pursued ambitious projects aimed at advancing the state-of-the-art in AI and addressing extraordinary scientific, engineering and societal challenges.

In 2024, hei founded and ran TrueMedia.org: http://truemedia.org/ - a non-profit technology organization using AI to create a non-partisan, free and broadly accessible dashboard to combat political disinformation by identifying manipulated media and AI-based forgery of video, audio, images and text across social media platforms. He has been a Professor at the University of Washington’s Computer Science department since 1991, and a Venture Partner at the Madrona Venture Group since 2000. He is also the Founder and Technical Director of the AI2 Incubator.

About the Raj Reddy Artificial Intelligence Lecture : The Raj Reddy Artificial Intelligence Lecture Series was established in honor of Raj Reddy and his critical work in AI during his now 50 year career at CMU. Reddy was the founding Director of the Robotics Institute from 1979 to 1991 and the Dean of School of Computer Science from 1991 to 1999. He has been active in AI research for over five decades in the areas of AI, Speech Understanding, Image Understanding, Robotics, Multi-sensor Fusion, and Intelligent Agents.
