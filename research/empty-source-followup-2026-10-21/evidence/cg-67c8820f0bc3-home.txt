			23 text Our club is dedicated to teaching students the skills needed to effectively protect themselves in situations where they lack a physical advantage. Our aim is to provide students with a foundational understanding of hand-to-hand combat, weapon familiarity, and techniques for defusing hazardous scenarios. Moreover, we'll instruct you on utilizing everyday household items, such as water bottles and umbrellas, as defensive tools in the event of an attack. While our curriculum is designed with a focus on women, we warmly welcome individuals of all genders to join us.
